### Q-M2 — "How would you go about opening BBVA as an account? Walk me through deal mechanics."

**Respuesta modelo.**

> BBVA's mechanics are: long sales cycle, high compliance bar, but once you're in, expansion is structural because banks consolidate vendors. So the play is **patient land, aggressive expand**.
>
> The entry isn't with the CIO and it isn't with credit risk — Palantir already has credit risk and I'm not going to lose six months competing with them on the wrong workflow. The entry is **collections or document recovery** with the COO of Consumer Finance or the Head of Collections. Land deal: a defined slice — say, soft collections on a specific product like consumer loans — three-month pilot, €120K-€180K, the customer pays for it because that disciplines the deployment. ROI target on the pilot is 8-10x within the period; HappyRobot has the 119x reference on collections globally, which lets me pitch confidently even at the lower end.
>
> Deal levers for the land deal: I'd hold the platform fee, discount the FDE deployment for the first engagement in exchange for **a co-marketing reference clause** and a multi-year option to renew at standard rates. That's the Palantir play — discount the first deal in exchange for the reference, never discount the recurring rate. The multi-year option means if we hit the ROI, the expansion is mechanically pre-priced.
>
> Expand sequence at month 9 once collections is live and producing: **customer service in retail banking** (volume is enormous, 8M+ customers in Spain), then **document recovery for SMB lending**, then **front-office triage for the corporate segment**. Each expansion is its own statement of work with its own deal levers — incremental per-action pricing, additional FDE allocation, channel expansion (voice + email + chat). My back-of-envelope path is **€150K land → €600K at month 12 → €2M+ multi-workflow at month 24-30**. The discipline is to never let the expansion conversation start with "buy more platform" — it always starts with the next workflow.

> Access channel: ICADE network — both BBVA's current CEO and several of their division heads went through ICADE in the same years as some of my classmates. That gets the second meeting, not the first. The first meeting comes via warm intro from someone who's already cleared procurement at BBVA — and that's where I'd want to use the Palantir reference asymmetrically: "your credit risk team has already worked with US enterprise AI vendors through procurement, you know the path".

**Talking points / vocab.**
- "Patient land, aggressive expand" — clean frame, no consulting-speak.
- Deal levers nombrados: platform fee, FDE deployment discount, co-marketing clause, multi-year option, per-action pricing.
- Expansion path con tres números (€150K → €600K → €2M) — eco directo del "30K → 300K → 3M" Pascal Unger / Palantir.
